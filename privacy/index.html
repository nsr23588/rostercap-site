1. Who we are

RosterCap is operated by Nathan Rischling. It is a native Mac application for roster, recruiting, budget, depth-chart, and team planning. You can create a workspace on your Mac without creating a separate RosterCap login. Your organization decides which people and information belong in its workspace.

For privacy or support requests, email capmp@innotrace.dev.

2. Information you put in RosterCap

Depending on the features you use, a workspace can contain:

  • Player, recruit, staff, and family contact details; roster identifiers, positions, eligibility and academic information; photographs or image links; notes and recruiting events.
  • Scholarship and other aid amounts, costs of attendance, budgets, and planning calculations. RosterCap does not request payment-card or bank-account credentials.
  • Depth charts, lineups, planning versions, workspace settings, and record-edit metadata.

Earlier versions could contain injury, availability, fitness, and wellness records entered by workspace users. Health features are removed from this beta. Previously stored Health records are preserved but are not exposed, uploaded, downloaded, or shared by this beta. RosterCap does not import information from HealthKit.

Enter information only when you have authority to do so. Your organization is responsible for the permissions needed to record or share athlete, family, financial, or minor-related information, including any Health information retained from earlier versions. RosterCap is intended for authorized staff use; athletes do not need to create an app account.

3. Local storage and optional iCloud

RosterCap saves workspace records on your Mac. Local-only workspaces are not uploaded to RosterCap’s CloudKit storage unless you enable synchronization for that workspace. RosterCap also retains synchronization metadata, pending edits, and competing record versions so interrupted work and conflicts can be handled without silently discarding a draft.

If you enable iCloud Sync, RosterCap uses Apple’s CloudKit service with your system Apple Account to store and synchronize the workspace. This includes the selected workspace’s roster, recruiting, administration, financial, and planning records. Health records are excluded from enrollment, uploads, downloads, and sharing in this beta. RosterCap receives an app-specific CloudKit account identifier and share metadata; it does not request your Apple Account password.

RosterCap does not operate a separate developer-hosted database for these native workspaces. The developer does not receive routine workspace copies through an analytics service. Apple operates iCloud and processes service and account information under its own terms and privacy policy. CloudKit uses Apple’s security mechanisms; RosterCap does not promise that every local file, metadata field, export, or recipient’s copy is end-to-end encrypted.

4. Sharing with colleagues

A workspace owner can invite colleagues through Apple’s sharing interface separately for Organization and Staff, Roster and Recruiting, Financials, and Official Depth Chart. A colleague with edit permission can change or delete records throughout the shared area. Job titles in RosterCap are descriptive and do not independently restrict share permissions.

Personal Planning is not offered as a team share. For a workspace owner it can synchronize privately; a participant’s personal planning is local to that participant’s Mac. Owners can manage participant access through the share controls. Removing access affects future access when the app learns of the change; it cannot recall screenshots, exported files, calendar entries, or other copies a recipient made.

5. Calendars, image hosts, and files

If you enable calendar integration, RosterCap requests macOS Calendar permission to read calendars and manage recruiting events. Events you place in a selected calendar can include recruiting details and follow that calendar provider’s own synchronization and sharing settings. You can revoke Calendar access in macOS System Settings.

When a player record contains a remote headshot URL, RosterCap may load that image as the player is displayed. The image host receives the request and normal connection information, such as the device’s network address and requested URL. Its own privacy practices apply. Avoid placing sensitive information in image URLs or using untrusted image hosts.

Files you explicitly select can be imported into the workspace. PDF reports and other files you save remain wherever you choose to save or share them, including any cloud-synchronized folder. These copies are managed separately from the workspace.

6. Support, diagnostics, and advertising

If you contact RosterCap support, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you send, and use them to respond and investigate the issue. Please use synthetic examples when possible and avoid sending real athlete financial or health records.

Support correspondence has no automatic deletion schedule. It may remain in support email records while it is useful for responding to your request or investigating the issue. You can ask us to remove correspondence you sent by emailing capmp@innotrace.dev. We review those requests individually; deleting our correspondence does not remove copies held by you, your organization, or an email provider.

RosterCap has no advertising SDK, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics SDK. Local diagnostic logs may contain technical errors and identifiers. Apple may provide installation, crash, or beta feedback information through TestFlight according to Apple’s processes and your choices. We use feedback we receive to troubleshoot and improve RosterCap.

7. Retention and deletion

Workspaces remain on your Mac until their stored data is removed. Archiving hides records from normal active views; it is not deletion. Deleting a record can retain a local deletion marker and its prior contents for synchronization. Pending changes and conflict versions can also remain locally. The current beta has no automatic expiry or secure-erasure schedule for this information.

Switching workspaces or signing out of iCloud does not erase the local database. The app keeps the old account’s workspace partition inaccessible to another account so unsynchronized drafts are not uploaded into that other account. For active synchronized areas, when CloudKit reports that a share or zone is unavailable, the app removes that area’s local synchronized cache. Dormant Health records from previous versions are preserved and are not changed in response to cloud events by this beta.

For corrections or deletion of an organization’s workspace records, contact its workspace owner first. The app’s edit, archive, and delete controls are not a complete data-erasure facility. For assistance locating and removing local RosterCap data, contact capmp@innotrace.dev before removing files, especially if you have unsynchronized work.

Health data already uploaded by an earlier version is not automatically removed by installing this beta. The developer cannot directly delete the contents of your private iCloud workspace on your behalf. Removing RosterCap alone should not be assumed to remove all local or iCloud records. Exports, backups, calendar entries, and copies on other devices or held by colleagues are separate and may remain until their holders or providers remove them. We cannot promise deletion of those independent copies.

8. This website

This informational website uses GitHub Pages for static hosting. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when a visitor is not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub’s Pages data-collection notice and GitHub’s privacy statement.

We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, account forms, or cookies to these pages. Images are served with the site, and text uses system fonts. Email links open your email application; sending an email shares the information described in the support section above. Links to other websites are governed by those websites’ privacy practices.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when RosterCap’s data practices change and revise its effective date. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to capmp@innotrace.dev.

For a request concerning someone else’s workspace, provide only enough information to identify the issue. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive records.

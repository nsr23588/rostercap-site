Technical support

Tell us what happened.

A useful report includes:

  • Your RosterCap version and macOS version.
  • The steps you took and what you expected to happen.
  • The error message, if there is one.
  • Whether the workspace is local-only or uses iCloud Sync.

Please remove personal details from screenshots and use made-up examples. Do not email athlete financial or health records, Apple Account passwords, or verification codes.

Data & privacy

Your workspace and copies.

Contact your workspace owner first to correct organization records or change colleague access. For help locating local app data or requesting removal of information you sent to support, email us.

Archiving a record, signing out of iCloud, or uninstalling RosterCap does not guarantee complete deletion. The beta has no automatic expiry or secure-erasure schedule. Exports, calendar entries, backups, and copies held by other people must be managed separately.

Health features are excluded from this beta. Earlier local and iCloud Health records are preserved; installing the beta does not delete them.

About the beta

RosterCap is being prepared for beta testing on macOS 27 or later. There is no public download or TestFlight invitation link on this site yet. Email support with questions about availability.
